These terms are consolidated by inferred intent, not by adding search-volume estimates together.

## Competitor observations

[MakeMyGraph tree tool](https://www.makemygraph.com/generate/Tree-Diagram) was inspected in the browser. Its title targets Tree Diagram Generator. Quick Start exposes Decision, Family Tree and generic presets. The inspected decision preset fills actual choice/outcome nodes. Controls include parent reassignment, sibling reordering, connection styles, zoom/fit and a Download menu listing PNG, SVG and PDF. Export availability was observed in the menu; downloaded files, account restrictions and save/share behavior were not verified. The inspected view is dominated by the editor and does not expose an explanatory guide or FAQ below it. This is an observation of that view, not proof of missing content across the domain or a ranking explanation.

Our response is six original editable presets, static previews with meaningful descriptions, a definition/example/how-to/FAQ, and confirmation before replacing work. Existing spreadsheet imports and local project backups remain available. We are not copying competitor node data, adding PDF, family genealogy, branch probabilities, reordering, zoom or new SEO URLs in this task.

Search-result samples also included [Visme](https://www.visme.co/tree-diagram-maker/), [Miro](https://miro.com/diagramming/tree-diagram/) and [draw.io](https://www.drawio.com/docs/diagram-types/tree-diagrams/). Their maker/creator/generator terminology supports the inference that these commercial phrases overlap. Broad “tree diagram” also includes educational probability intent; the page clearly states that our decision outlines do not perform probability calculations.

## Original presets

Blank hierarchy; project plan; organization chart with one reporting parent; decision outline with choices in node labels; website structure; classification tree. No separate near-duplicate template routes. Real static fragment targets work without client JavaScript, while the shared editor reads recognized fragments to offer a confirmed replacement.

The publisher's [TreeDraw page](https://www.spansoft.org/product/treedraw/) confirms that TreeDraw is also the name of genealogy charting software. This reinforces treating the short phrase tree draw as ambiguous.
